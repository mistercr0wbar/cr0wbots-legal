This page covers the **BARBIE** adult Discord application. BARNEY has a

The public support server is SFW. Do not post adult profile details, sexual
content, intimate images or private consent disputes there.

## Ordinary bugs

For an ordinary technical problem, include:

- the Discord guild ID;
- the command or feature used;
- what you expected and what happened; and
- the approximate time and time zone.

Use neutral descriptions. A bug report does not need anyone's kinks, safeword,
sexual orientation, private messages or intimate media.

## Privacy and data requests

Email **cr0wbotsupport@gmail.com** with the subject
`BARBIE privacy request`. Include:

- your Discord user ID;
- whether you want access, correction, export or deletion; and
- a short description of the record involved.

The operator may ask for a reasonable confirmation through Discord to prevent
one person from requesting another person's data. Do not send government ID,
passwords, tokens, nude images, sexual evidence or unrelated private messages.

The current `profile delete` prefix action clears Bot Play and D/s opt-ins but
does not erase the written profile. Use the email route above for permanent
deletion until an in-app deletion command is available.

## Consent, harassment and safety reports

For a private report about abuse of BARBIE's consent controls, use email with
the subject `BARBIE safety report`. Provide the smallest amount of information
needed to identify the account, guild and feature.

Use Discord's in-app reporting tools for violations of Discord's rules,
including sexual exploitation, grooming, non-consensual intimate media,
harassment or threats. If there is an immediate threat to someone's physical
safety, contact the appropriate local emergency service. BARBIE support is not
an emergency or real-time monitoring service.

## Security reports

Report vulnerabilities privately by email with the subject
`BARBIE security report`. Include enough technical detail to reproduce the
issue without including live credentials, intimate content or other people's
personal information.

Do not publish an unpatched exploit, access data that is not yours, disrupt the
service or test against guilds without authorization.

## Never send

- nudes or sexually explicit evidence;
- government identification;
- Discord passwords, user tokens or bot tokens;
- `.env` files or database credentials;
- medical information;
- large exports of private messages; or
- identifying information about a minor beyond what Discord or lawful
  authorities specifically require through their own reporting process.

No guaranteed response time or uptime is offered. Requests are handled as
reasonably as possible based on urgency and available information.
