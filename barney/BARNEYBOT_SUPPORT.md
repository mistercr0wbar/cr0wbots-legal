# BARNEY Support

**Email:** cr0wbotsupport@gmail.com

**Support server:** https://discord.gg/qkGVG3daZS

This page covers the **BARNEY** Discord application. BARBIE has a separate
[adult-app support page](../barbie/BARBIEBOT_SUPPORT.md).

## Before contacting support

For an ordinary bug, include:

- the Discord guild ID, or say that the problem occurred in a DM;
- the command or feature used;
- what you expected and what happened;
- the approximate time and time zone; and
- any Discord error message that was shown.

Screenshots are optional. Remove unrelated names and messages before sending
them.

## Privacy and data requests

Email **cr0wbotsupport@gmail.com** with a subject such as
`BARNEY privacy request`. Include:

- your Discord user ID;
- the guild ID if the request concerns a guild;
- whether you want access, correction, export or deletion; and
- if requesting guild data, how you are authorized to act for that guild.

The operator may ask for a reasonable confirmation through Discord to prevent
one person from requesting another person's data. Do not send government ID,
Discord passwords, authentication tokens or unrelated private messages.

BARNEY cannot directly erase copies that a guild has chosen to post into its
Discord log or transcript channels. Contact that guild's administrators or
Discord about those copies.

## Security reports

Report vulnerabilities privately by email with the subject
`BARNEY security report`. Include enough detail to reproduce the issue without
including live credentials or other people's personal information.

Do not publish an unpatched exploit, access data that is not yours, disrupt the
service or test against guilds without authorization.

## Safety and Discord violations

Use Discord's in-app reporting tools for violations of Discord's rules. If
there is an immediate threat to someone's physical safety, contact the
appropriate local emergency service. BARNEY support is not an emergency or
real-time monitoring service.

## Never send

- bot or user tokens;
- passwords or `.env` files;
- payment-card or banking information;
- government identification;
- private verification evidence; or
- large exports of other people's messages.

No guaranteed response time or uptime is offered. Requests are handled as
reasonably as possible based on urgency and available information.
